BridgeLite is a lightweight macOS viewer built for one job: deciding which photos to keep.

After a big shoot you can be left with thousands of RAW+JPG frames. Lightroom and Bridge are powerful, but heavy to launch just to cull. BridgeLite fills that gap — it shows you the information you need to pick the keepers, as fast and accurately as possible.

• RAW+JPG as one shot — frames from the same shutter are grouped and toggled with a single tap, so you compare JPG, RAW and retouched variations seamlessly.
• Never modifies your files — browsing, scrolling and filtering never touch the original bytes, timestamps or permissions. Only ratings are written, to XMP sidecar files. Your RAW stays untouched.
• Native macOS speed — Image I/O, Grand Central Dispatch and an SQLite thumbnail cache deliver fast thumbnails and smooth 60 fps scrolling, even on folders with thousands of images.
• Read-only friendly — safe to browse straight from memory cards or network drives.
• Filter and analyze — sort and filter by EXIF (camera, lens, ISO, shutter, aperture) to zero in on the frames that matter.

BridgeLite does not develop, edit, resize or export. Those are jobs for Lightroom, Capture One or Darkroom. BridgeLite does one thing: help you choose, fast.
