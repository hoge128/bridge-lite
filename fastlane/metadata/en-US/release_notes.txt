Choosing the one frame to keep is the slow part of photography — especially after a long shoot, when a dozen near-identical shots all look the same. This update is about making that choice faster and easier.

Filmstrip — for when you can't decide between similar shots
Put any 2–4 photos side by side so the small differences (focus, expression, a blink) stand out, and pick your keeper with confidence.
• Build and rearrange the comparison set right from the viewer
• Redesigned preview with optional metadata and a resizable picker (grid or single row)

Wider RAW support — review straight off the card
Shoot RAW and cull from the real RAW, not a JPG. Embedded previews now read correctly from many more cameras:
• Apple ProRAW / iPhone DNG, Nikon Z6 III, Olympus OM-5 Mark II, and medium-format Hasselblad and Phase One
• Added Leica RWL and Canon CRW
• RAW with no usable preview now shows a clear placeholder

Smoother scrolling — for the thousand-photo folder
The thumbnail grid was reworked to hold a steady frame rate and re-decode less as you scroll, so nothing slows you down while skimming a big shoot.

Flag and filter your selects — decisions that stick
• Pick / Reject flags (P = keep, X = cut), saved as Adobe-compatible XMP so they carry into Lightroom and Bridge
• Midnight-pivot time filter, so a session that runs past midnight stays together
• New focal length / 35mm-equivalent filters and metadata, plus exposure compensation (EV)

Also
• Keyboard Shortcuts sheet, including Filmstrip and selection-mode shortcuts
• Filename search in Filmstrip
• Fixed an issue where opening a thumbnail could show a photo from a previous folder
• Various refinements and fixes
