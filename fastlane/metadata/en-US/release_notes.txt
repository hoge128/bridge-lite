Wider RAW support makes culling even smoother.

Wider RAW support — review straight off the card
Shoot RAW and cull from the real RAW, not a JPG. Embedded previews now read correctly from many more cameras:
• Added Olympus ORF (incl. OM-5 Mark II), Canon CRW, and Leica RWL
• Now supports medium-format Hasselblad (3FR/FFF) and Phase One (IIQ), Nikon Z6 III, and Apple ProRAW / iPhone DNG
• RAW with no usable preview now shows a clear "No preview" placeholder

Better filtering and sorting
• Added a time-of-day filter with a midnight pivot, so a session that runs past midnight stays together
• Added "Date Taken" sorting (by EXIF capture time)
• Filter category chips now extend to the edge of the screen

Also
• Fixed an issue where, after opening a different folder, a photo from the previous folder could occasionally appear
• Various refinements and fixes
