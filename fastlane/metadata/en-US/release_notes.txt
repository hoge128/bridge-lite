A stability and speed release.

Fixed: crash when dragging photos
• Dragging a photo out of BridgeLite (to Finder, Mail, or another app) could crash the app on some Macs. This is fixed

Share straight from the grid
• Right-click any photo and choose Share to send it to any app or service — no need to drag out to Finder first
• Copying, dragging, or sharing a photo now brings its XMP sidecar along automatically, so ratings, labels, and captions travel with the file

Filtering that keeps up with you
• Toggling a rating or label filter now highlights instantly, even on large libraries — the actual re-filtering runs in the background with a shimmer showing it's still working
• Smoother dragging on the ISO / aperture / shutter speed / focal length histograms

Smoother Mission Control
• Fixed a stutter when entering Mission Control or switching spaces with BridgeLite open, most noticeable on large libraries
• Background animations and diagnostic timers now pause while BridgeLite isn't active, reducing idle load

Also
• Various refinements and stability fixes
