## Description (4,000 chars max)

BridgeLite is a fast photo culling app built for photographers who shoot raw + jpg simultaneously.

Designed for high-volume shooting — events, travel, sports, weddings — BridgeLite helps you quickly decide which shots to keep. After culling, hand off your picks to Lightroom or any other editing app in seconds.

**Treat raw + jpg as one shot**
Automatically pairs raw and jpg files from the same shot. Switch between them seamlessly with one tap to compare before rating.

**Fast Thumbnail Browsing**
Prioritizes embedded thumbnails for instant loading, even in folders with hundreds of shots. Scroll smoothly and filter on the fly.

**Star Ratings & Color Labels**
Rate shots 1–5 stars and apply color labels, written to XMP sidecar files. Fully compatible with Lightroom, Capture One, and other professional tools.

**Powerful Filtering**
Filter by aperture, shutter speed, focal length, rating, file type, or date — focus on exactly what you need, like showing only unrated shots to work through a backlog efficiently.

**Requirements & Notes**
To browse photos on an SD card, a Lightning or USB-C SD card reader adapter is required (sold separately). Files accessible through the standard Files app can also be browsed directly. Please note that reading from SD cards or low-speed connections such as USB 2.0 ports may result in reduced performance due to transfer speed limitations. For best performance, iPhone Pro models with USB 3.0 are recommended.
